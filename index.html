Not Mad Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Not Mad ("the app") lets you check in with the people in your circle so they know you're not mad at them. This page explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Phone number. Used to sign you in with a one-time text code. We never show your number to other users.
  • Display name and time zone. Your name is shown to people in your circle. Your time zone decides when your day starts and ends.
  • Circle and check-ins. Who is in your circle, invite codes, and when you tap "not mad". People in your circle see their own status with you.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token to send check-in reminders.
  • Purchases. If you buy a subscription or add-on, we receive the purchase status from Apple through our payment provider. We never see your card or Apple ID details.

Contacts

If you allow access, the app reads your contacts on your device only to open a contact card so you can text or call someone. Your contacts are never uploaded to our servers.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase stores your account and app data.
  • An SMS provider sends your sign-in codes.
  • Expo and Apple deliver push notifications.
  • RevenueCat and Apple process purchases.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use it for tracking across other apps or websites.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete account, which removes your profile, circle, and check-ins. Purchase records may be kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for billing.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
