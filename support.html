Not Mad Support

Something wrong? We're not mad. Probably.

Contact

Email support@prather.org with your question and, if it's about your account, the display name you use in the app. We usually reply within a few days.

How does checking in work?

Tap the "not mad" button once a day for each person whose circle you're in. Your day resets at midnight in your own time zone.

What do the colors mean?

Green means you checked in today. Yellow means you haven't yet. Red means a full day passed without a tap. Gray means paused. That's all the app knows; it can't detect actual feelings.

I'm not getting reminders

Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Not Mad and make sure notifications are allowed. You can change reminder times inside the app.

How do I take a break?

In the app, go to Settings → Not mad at and tap Pause next to the person's name. While paused you show up as gray, not red. Tap Resume to start again.

How do I leave someone's circle?

In the app, go to Settings → Not mad at and tap Leave next to the person's name. They'll see that you left, but no reason is given.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Not Mad, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Can people tell if I use Auto-Not-Mad?

Yes, if they pay for it. People whose circles you're in can buy a feature that shows your check-in history and which check-ins were automatic.

I bought something and it's missing

In the app, go to Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't show up, email us. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, circles, and check-ins. Deleting the account doesn't cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel that first.
